Why AgrowAssist

Farmers often have to make important decisions without having all the information they need, from choosing the right crop to identifying plant diseases and checking the freshness of their produce. AgrowAssist aims to make these decisions easier with four AI-powered tools: crop recommendations based on soil and weather conditions, disease identification through images, fruit and vegetable recognition with nutritional information, and freshness detection to distinguish healthy produce from rotten ones.

The mobile app came first. This website is the same models, the same data, rebuilt as a browser-based platform — so the tools work without an install.
