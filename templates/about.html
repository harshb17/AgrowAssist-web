Why AgrowAssist

Farmers make decisions with incomplete information — what to plant, whether a crop is diseased, whether produce is still sellable. AgrowAssist puts four AI models behind those decisions: crop recommendation from soil and climate data, disease detection from a single photo, produce identification with nutrition data, and a healthy-vs-rotten check.

The mobile app came first. This website is the same models, the same data, rebuilt as a browser-based platform — so the tools work without an install.
